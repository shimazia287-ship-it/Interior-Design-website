Our Process

Four movements, one
considered whole.

Every commission follows the same discipline — a rhythm that keeps ambition and clarity in balance from first conversation to final detail.

01 ◌

Discovery

We begin with conversation — how you live, what you gather, what you want to leave behind.

02 ◇

Concept & Design

Spatial plans, elemental palettes, and material stories become a coherent architectural direction.

03 ▦

Curation

We source furnishings, commissions, and objects that complete the room without competing with it.

04 ⌕

Delivery

We oversee every detail, bringing the final environment to life with precision and restraint.

FAQ

Questions,
answered.

Everything you might want to know before we begin. Still curious? Get in touch.

What is the minimum project scope you take on?

We typically begin with full-service residential or hospitality projects, but we also consider focused rooms and design consultations depending on the brief.

How is your fee structured?

Fees are tailored to the scope, complexity, location, and level of involvement required.

Do you work outside of London?

Yes. We work across the UK and internationally with a trusted network of contractors, artisans, and suppliers.

Can you collaborate with our architect or builder?

Absolutely. We regularly collaborate with architects, builders, lighting designers, landscape teams, and specialist makers.

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines vary by project, but a complete residential interior can take several months from concept through installation.
